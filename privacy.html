What we collect

Registration details you choose to give: your name, email and the answers relevant to your path (business, charity or supporter). Enquiry messages sent through the contact form.

Two separate permissions

Permission to respond: lets us reply to your registration or enquiry. Permission for updates: lets us send occasional news. You can give one without the other, and changing one does not change the other.

The contact form

Sending an enquiry does not register you and does not subscribe you to updates.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw a permission at any time by contacting us.
